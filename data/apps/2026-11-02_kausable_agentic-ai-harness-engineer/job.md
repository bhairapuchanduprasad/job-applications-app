At kausable, we build causal, reasoning-first models that learn from a handful of examples and generalize across domains. Research gets us to a capable, versatile foundation model. It's great at deducing the mechanics that govern a system; yet, standalone, it's just one query-answer cycle. 

This role harnesses the full potential of that core intelligence by embedding it in a sophisticated agentic system capable of tackling multi-step, long-horizon tasks.

As our Harness Engineer, you own the path from a raw ML model to an intelligent, largely autonomous system: harnesses, inference algorithmics, context curation, and hierarchical querying. You will work in the seam between research and product, giving free reign to your creativity when designing the system and exercising discipline and diligence when handing it over to the productization team.

Tasks

- Turn research models into sophisticated agentic systems capable of a large degree of autonomy.

- Build harnesses, inference algorithms and systems, smart context curation.

- Build release criteria that show quantitatively when an agent is ready to ship.

- Build developer-facing abstractions around our agentic systems.

- Work closely with researchers to expose failure modes; receive product feedback from customers and turn it into better systems.

- Translate customer and design-partner needs into reusable platform capabilities rather than one-off solutions.

Requirements

- A track record of ML-research or strong, research-adjacent ML engineering.

- Experience with in-context learning, building agentic systems, applying ML models in complex, dynamic environments.

- Strong software engineering skills in Python and hands-on fluency with PyTorch.

- Sound judgment around evaluation, observability, reliability and production trade-offs.

- The ability to work directly with customers, researchers and product stakeholders.

- A pragmatic, outcome-oriented mindset: you optimize for dependable capabilities that users can actually adopt.

- We are primarily hiring at senior level. We are also open to exceptional candidates with fewer years of experience who can demonstrate comparable depth, judgment and ownership.

Nice to have:

- PFNs, synthetic data or probabilistic models.  

- Weights & Biases, model registries, CI for models or comparable MLOps tooling.

- SDK or developer-tooling design.

- Prior startup, design-partner or 0-to-1 product experience.

- Own model releases, monitoring and rollback patterns as the production footprint grows. 

- Experience with model serving, APIs, containers and cloud infrastructure.

You will define how kausable ships ML: the patterns, tooling and standards between research and production. The trade-off is part of the job: shipping quickly matters, but only when the resulting system remains measurable, reusable and dependable.
