Purpose & Overall Relevance For The Organization

The Data & AI function includes an Advanced Analytics – Finance team that works closely with the Global Finance organization on strategically relevant use cases with the potential to deliver substantial value and improve the way Finance operates. Examples include a financial forecasting engine designed to make forecasting faster, more efficient and more responsive, as well as agentic AI products that help Finance users access trusted information, analyze data, automate multi-step workflows, and support decision-making within clearly defined governance and human-oversight boundaries.

As a Data Analyst in the Advanced Analytics – Finance team, you will support the development and continuous improvement of forecasting, agentic AI and other analytics solutions. Working with more experienced data scientists, Finance colleagues and Tech partners, you will contribute across the analytics lifecycle, from business and data understanding through data preparation, modelling and evaluation to deployment and monitoring. The role provides an opportunity to build and develop strong practical data science skills while contributing to measurable business outcomes.
