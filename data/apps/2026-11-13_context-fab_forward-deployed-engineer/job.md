About the Role:
We’re building 
context/fab, a venture-backed industrial AI startup building the 
agentic operating system for the future of manufacturing.
Our vision is the 
self-aware factory: factories that understand their own equipment, processes, data and operational context, and where AI agents can reason across this context to solve real manufacturing problems.
context/fab automatically connects fragmented industrial data and knowledge into a 
Context Graph, providing the foundation for agents across production, quality, maintenance, logistics and engineering.
We work directly with 
large industrial enterprises and leading SMEs, bringing this technology into real factories and operational workflows.
As our 
Forward Deployed Engineer, you’ll work directly with customers from discovery through deployment and expansion. You’ll understand how their factories operate, identify high-value problems, connect their systems and data to context/fab, and configure and deploy agents that deliver measurable operational impact.

What you will do:

- 
Work directly with customers to understand their factories, workflows, systems and most valuable operational problems
- Lead technical discovery, workshops and use-case definition together with customer operations, engineering and IT teams
- Translate manufacturing problems into agents and custom capabilities for the customer and measurable success criteria
- 
Build, configure and deploy agents and workflows for production, quality, maintenance, logistics and engineering use cases
- Connect customer data across PLCs and industrial protocols such as OPC UA and MQTT, SCADA and historians, MES, ERP, QMS, CMMS, databases, APIs and technical documentation

- Work closely with customers during pilots and rollouts, troubleshoot issues and continuously improve the solution
- Identify recurring customer needs and translate field learnings into clear requirements for our product and engineering teams
- Support technical sales and solution discovery for new customers
