As an 
Analytics Engineer (f/m/d), you will play a key role in connecting data engineering and analytics at KoRo. You will develop reliable data models, establish consistent metrics and create the foundation for accurate reporting and AI-driven insights. Working closely with Data, Tech and business teams, you will ensure a shared understanding of business data and enable teams to access and use data independently.

What you will do: 

- Data Modeling & Transformation: You design and maintain scalable dbt models and pipelines in BigQuery, ensuring high data quality through automated testing, performance optimization, and GDPR-compliant data processing. 
- Semantic Layer & Metrics: You build and maintain KoRo's semantic layer with consistent business metrics and AI-ready metadata, modeling key business entities and their relationships through ontologies and knowledge graphs. 
- AI Quality & Optimization: You evaluate and improve AI-generated answers using real business questions, identifying data inconsistencies, resolving modeling gaps, and preventing recurring errors. 
- Data Dictionary & Governance: You maintain a centralized, versioned data dictionary, working with Data and business teams to ensure consistent definitions of metrics, entities, and business rules across reporting and analytics. 
- Self-Service Analytics & Collaboration: You work closely with business stakeholders to make reliable data accessible through BI and AI tools, support user adoption, and enable teams to independently answer business questions. 

What you bring: 

- 3+ years of experience in analytics engineering, data engineering, or a similar role, ideally within e-commerce, consumer apps, or a fast-paced digital business. 
- Strong technical skills in SQL, dbt, and Python, with experience in data modeling, automated testing, and BI tools such as Tableau. Experience with GCP and BigQuery is a plus. 
- Hands-on experience with semantic layers and AI-ready data structures, including preparing data and context for LLM-based applications, ideally paired with experience evaluating AI-generated outputs. 
- Understanding of business ontologies and knowledge graphs, with experience modeling business entities and their relationships or a strong interest in developing expertise in this area. 
- Strong analytical and communication skills in English, with a business-oriented mindset, a focus on data quality and documentation, and the ability to align stakeholders on shared definitions and drive the adoption of data tools.
