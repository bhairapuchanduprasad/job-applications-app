Your Mission

As an 
AI Systems & Automation Engineer in our Platform team, you are a software engineer first—and an AI power-user in everything you build. Your mission is to turn critical operational workflows into robust, containerized production web services powering our internal teams and external customer touchpoints (such as customer onboarding and KYC flows).

You will leverage LLMs both in 
how you build (accelerating delivery with agentic coding tools) and

Your Responsibilities

- Professionalize Workflows into Production Web Services: Take manual, ad-hoc, or brittle processes (such as Google Forms, spreadsheet workflows, or fragile low-code scripts) and re-architect them into resilient, version-controlled web services with clean APIs, databases, robust authentication, and CI/CD pipelines.
- Build Customer-Facing & Platform Satellite Tools: Develop and run operational applications used directly by customers and internal operations—including customer onboarding portals, automated approval engines, document/KYC verification pipelines, and administrative services that support the core enmacc platforms.
- Engineer AI-Powered & Agentic Systems: Design and implement LLM-powered services, multi-step agentic workflows, and custom Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers that safely connect LLMs to our internal APIs, SaaS tools, and databases.
- Lead by Example in Agentic Software Engineering: Heavily utilize AI coding agents to deliver full-stack and backend systems rapidly—while championing best practices, reusable skills/prompts, and agentic engineering workflows across the wider engineering team.
- "You Build It, You Run It": Own the full lifecycle of your services from stakeholder problem discovery and architectural design to automated testing, containerized deployment, monitoring, and iteration.

- Proven Software Engineering Track Record: You bring 3–5+ years of professional software engineering experience building, testing, and operating production backend or full-stack web services (strong proficiency in Java, Python, TypeScript/Node.js, or Go).

- Production LLM & Agentic Integration Skills: You have hands-on experience integrating major LLM APIs (Anthropic Claude, OpenAI, Google Gemini) into real software projects, using structured outputs, function/tool calling, RAG patterns, evaluation frameworks, and agent protocols (such as MCP).
