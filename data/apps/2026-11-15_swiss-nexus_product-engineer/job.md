We’re hiring a Product Engineer for a Series A startup building voice AI software for consumer businesses. The platform lets customers launch and manage voice agents at scale.
Engineers own the product here. You’ll talk to customers, decide what to build, write the code, and see how it works once it’s live. There’s no dedicated PM handing you a spec. The team works together in Berlin, pairs often, and expects you to ship something to production on your first day.

What you’ll do

- Talk to customers and turn what you learn into features you can ship and test quickly.
- Build across the stack, from the interface customers use to configure their agents to the backend services that run them.
- Decide what needs to be solid from day one and what can improve once customers have used it.
- Follow your work into production, fix what breaks, and use customer feedback to decide what to do next.

What we’re looking for

- Full-stack engineering experience at a startup, with a clear example of a product or feature you took from idea to production.
- Good product judgment. You can explain what you chose to build, why it mattered, and what changed for users.
- Strong engineering fundamentals and the ability to get up to speed in an unfamiliar codebase. We use TypeScript and Python.
- A strong academic background. We’re especially interested in what you’ve built on your own initiative, whether at work or on the side.
- Availability to work in person in Berlin full-time.
