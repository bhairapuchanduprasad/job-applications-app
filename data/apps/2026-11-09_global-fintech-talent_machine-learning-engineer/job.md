Machine learning engineer (AI) 
Remote | Production machine learning | Competitive salary.

Are you a machine learning engineer who wants to see your models solving real problems for millions of users, rather than remaining experiments in a notebook?

We're recruiting for a well-funded AI technology company developing a new generation of intelligent consumer applications. Its goal is to create proactive AI assistants that understand context, learn from user behaviour and help people manage everyday tasks with minimal prompting.

The business is building an AI-native mobile application where classification, intelligent search, recommendations and personalisation are central to the user experience.

You'll join a remote-first engineering team with substantial financial backing and the opportunity to take ownership of machine learning systems from development through to production.

This is a hands-on machine learning engineering position focused on building and improving production models, not a purely research-oriented role.

The role: You'll develop and improve the machine learning systems that enable the application to understand information, retrieve relevant context and deliver personalised experiences.

Your responsibilities will include:

- Building ML models for classification, ranking, retrieval, recommendations and personalisation,
- Developing efficient classifiers and improving accuracy, precision, inference speed and cost,
- Optimising search relevance through embeddings, vector search, hybrid retrieval and reranking,
- Developing personalisation models using real-world data while respecting user privacy,
- Creating evaluation pipelines, labelled datasets and measurable quality metrics,
- Fine-tuning and adapting existing models where appropriate,
- Building and maintaining training, inference and production deployment pipelines,
- Monitoring model performance, identifying regressions and improving results based on real-world feedback.

What we're looking for: We're particularly interested in engineers who have successfully taken machine learning models beyond experimentation and into production.

Ideally, you'll bring:

- Strong Python programming skills and experience with PyTorch and/or JAX,
- Solid foundations in machine learning and modern neural network architectures,
- Hands-on experience developing, deploying and maintaining production ML models,
- Practical experience with classification, ranking, information retrieval, recommendation systems or personalisation,
- Understanding of embeddings, vector search and relevant ML evaluation techniques,
- Experience building training and inference pipelines using real-world datasets,
- Ability to balance model quality, latency, infrastructure costs and reliability,
- Strong engineering ownership and an analytical approach to problem-solving.

We're looking for practical machine learning engineers who can explain not just how a model works, but why they selected it, how they evaluated it and what happened when it reached production.

What we need to see in your CV: 
Please give specific examples of models you've personally developed, evaluated and deployed.

Your CV should describe:

- 
Production ML systems: Models you've built and deployed, their intended use cases and whether they're currently used in production,
- 
Technical stack: Your experience with Python, PyTorch/JAX, embeddings, retrieval systems, vector databases and ML infrastructure,
- 
ML expertise: Practical work involving classification, ranking, recommendation systems, retrieval or personalisation,
- 
Evaluation methods: Metrics used to assess model quality, such as precision, recall, F1, NDCG, relevance or other appropriate measures,
- 
Measurable results: Improvements in model accuracy, retrieval quality, inference latency, operating costs or production reliability, with figures where available,
- 
Personal ownership: Your contribution to data preparation, model development, training, experimentation, deployment and ongoing monitoring,
- 
Practical information: Employment dates, current location, current and expected annual compensation with currency, and notice period.

Research projects and prototypes are relevant, but please distinguish these from models you've successfully deployed and operated in production.

Why join?

- Work on an ambitious AI-native product with global consumer potential,
- Join an early-stage technology business backed by substantial investment,
- Take ownership of practical machine learning challenges with direct product impact,
- Build and improve models that will influence real user experiences,
- Work remotely alongside experienced engineering and product specialists,
- Receive competitive compensation combining salary and equity.

Application and selection process: Your CV will first be assessed for hands-on machine learning experience, production ownership and evidence of measurable results.

Applicants passing CV screening will normally be invited to complete an online technical coding assessment. The assessment is completed remotely, monitored for integrity and reviewed by the client's technical engineering team.

Successful applicants progress to technical and leadership interviews. Exceptional profiles may be fast-tracked directly to a technical interview.

Please provide sufficient detail in your CV to demonstrate your individual contributions and production ML experience.

Interested in building machine learning systems that make AI genuinely useful in everyday life? We'd like to hear from you.
