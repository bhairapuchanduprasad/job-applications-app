A Snapshot of Your Day 

As an AI Solutions Specialist (f/m/d) - Business Applications, Agents & Automation, you work at the intersection of sales operations, AI, and digital transformation within Global Sales Operations. Your day may start with transforming a business idea into a rapid prototype and continue with connecting enterprise systems, data sources, and business applications to enable seamless data flow. You collaborate closely with stakeholders, support citizen developers through workshops and reusable solutions, and help teams adopt AI-driven applications and automation. At the same time, you ensure governance, security, and compliance standards are met. Your work turns innovative concepts into secure, scalable solutions that create lasting value for the sales organization.

How You’ll Make An Impact

- Responsibility for designing, prototyping, and implementing AI-driven solutions, intelligent agents, and automated workflows for sales operations
- Responsibility for connecting enterprise systems, data sources, and business applications to enable seamless integration and data flow
- Implementation of rapid prototypes to validate digital opportunities and transition successful concepts into scalable production-ready applications
- Support for citizen developers through technical guidance, reusable templates, and best-practice enablement
- Coordination of requirements translation from complex sales business needs into technical specifications and digital solutions
- Ensuring governance, security, data privacy compliance, and lifecycle management for business-led applications and AI use cases

What You Bring

- Degree in Computer Science, Business Informatics, or a related field
- Experience in developing business applications, AI solutions, or automation workflows within an enterprise environment
- Knowledge of AI Agent Design, Prompt Engineering, Robotic Process Automation (RPA), Workflow Automation, API integration, data connectors, database management, and data modeling
- Proficiency in Low-Code/No-Code Platform Development, including platforms such as Power Platform, combined with experience in solution architecture, agile project management, and rapid prototyping
- Strong capability in requirements engineering, governance, security, data privacy, and lifecycle management for decentralized applications
- Fluency in English and a collaborative, solution-oriented mindset with the ability to work effectively with diverse stakeholders and drive digital transformation initiatives

Who is Siemens Energy? 

At Siemens Energy, we are more than just an energy technology company. With ~100.000 dedicated employees in more than 90 countries, we develop the energy systems of the future, ensuring that the growing energy demand of the global community is met reliably and sustainably. The technologies created in our research departments and factories drive the energy transition and provide the base for one sixth of the world's electricity generation.

Our global team is committed to making sustainable, reliable, and affordable energy a reality by pushing the boundaries of what is possible. We uphold a 150-year legacy of innovation that encourages our search for people who will support our focus on decarbonization, new technologies, and energy transformation.
