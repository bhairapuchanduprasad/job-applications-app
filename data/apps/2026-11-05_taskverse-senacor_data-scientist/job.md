About The Company

Senacor Technologies AG is a technology and consulting company that supports organizations in addressing complex digital and business challenges. The company works at the intersection of strategy, business, and technology, helping clients develop and implement solutions for demanding, data-intensive environments. Senacor brings together people with different areas of expertise to deliver practical outcomes and create lasting value for its clients.

About The Role

As a Data Scientist (m/f/d) at Senacor, you will turn data into insights, models, and solutions that help address complex challenges. You will work with statistical methods, modern analytical tools, and advanced technologies to explore large and varied datasets. From identifying promising analytical approaches to developing and evaluating algorithms, you will contribute to data-driven projects and help clients make better-informed decisions.

This role combines analytical thinking with practical implementation. You will collaborate with colleagues and stakeholders to understand business questions, translate them into data science problems, and communicate findings in a clear and useful way. Your work may involve preparing data, testing hypotheses, building models, and refining solutions based on results and feedback. The specific project context will shape your priorities, giving you the opportunity to apply your skills to a range of data-related challenges.

Qualifications

You have a degree in data science, computer science, statistics, mathematics, a quantitative discipline, or comparable practical experience. You are enthusiastic about innovative technologies and advanced analytics, and you are interested in applying data science to real-world problems.

You bring sound knowledge of statistical methods and experience working with datasets, analytical models, or algorithms. Familiarity with programming languages and tools used for data analysis and model development is valuable. You approach problems systematically, question assumptions, and pay attention to the quality and interpretation of data. You can explain technical concepts and analytical results clearly to people with different levels of technical expertise.

We also value curiosity, initiative, and a willingness to learn. You enjoy working collaboratively, can adapt to changing project needs, and are comfortable balancing detailed analysis with a focus on practical outcomes. Professional English skills are important for collaboration in an international business environment; German language skills may be beneficial depending on the project.

Responsibilities

In this position, you will analyze complex datasets to identify patterns, trends, and opportunities. You will develop, test, and refine data-driven models and algorithms, selecting methods that fit the problem and the available data. You will prepare and validate data, assess the performance and limitations of analytical approaches, and document your work so that results can be understood and used effectively.

You will work with project teams and stakeholders to clarify requirements, turn business needs into analytical questions, and present findings and recommendations. You will contribute to solving data-intensive challenges using modern tools and technologies, while considering data quality, responsible use, and the needs of the intended users. As projects progress, you will incorporate feedback and help improve models, processes, and solutions.

Benefits

At Senacor, you can contribute to challenging technology and data projects alongside colleagues from a range of disciplines. The role offers opportunities to deepen your expertise, learn modern analytical approaches, and apply your skills to practical client challenges. You will have the chance to take ownership of meaningful work, exchange ideas with experienced team members, and develop your capabilities as projects and technologies evolve. Specific benefits and working arrangements may vary by role and location.
