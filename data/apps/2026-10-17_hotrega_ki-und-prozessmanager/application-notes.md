## Likely interview questions (prepare in German too)
1. **"Welche Prozesse würden Sie bei uns zuerst automatisieren?"** (Which processes would you automate first?)
   Ask first, then propose: repeat product questions from retailers and customers (answers from data sheets), creating data sheets and hygiene plans from templates, order and complaint emails sorted by category. Start with a human approving every answer, measure accuracy, then automate more.
2. **"Wie stellen Sie sicher, dass die KI keine falschen Produktinformationen gibt?"** (How do you make sure the AI doesn't give wrong product information?)
   Answers only from sources (RAG with citations), a labelled test set, a confidence threshold with a person in the loop. Kniit: tested against SAP ground truth (P/R 33%/45% → 93%/90%); Covestro: chemists' ground truth.
3. **"Was wissen Sie über den EU AI Act und Datenschutz bei KI?"** (What do you know about the EU AI Act and data protection?)
   Honest basics: risk-based categories; transparency duties (label AI-generated content, inform people when they talk to a bot); GDPR: data minimisation, no personal data in prompts without a legal basis, data processing agreements with LLM providers, EU hosting where possible. Say you'd keep a simple register of AI use cases.

Also prepare: n8n basics (nodes, triggers, credentials), Microsoft Copilot / Copilot Studio at overview level, why you left Kniit (public line, German version in the profile).
