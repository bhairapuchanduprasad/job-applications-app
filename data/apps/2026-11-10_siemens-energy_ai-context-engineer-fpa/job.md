A Snapshot of Your Day 

You're building the foundation of the Financial Planning & Analysis of tomorrow. As AI & Context Engineer FP&A you lead the Context Workstream for FP&A: you build the FP&A context layer and its governance and operating model, and the agents that run FP&A processes end to end. The context layer reaches from extracting and structuring the knowledge in our documents, processes and definitions, through the semantic layer that makes it binding, to retrieval that every AI tool can consume, commodity or self-built. On that foundation you make FP&A processes agentic: together with our process analysts you decompose redesigned processes into steps an agent can execute and build the agents that run them. You are part of the FP&A Transformation & Enablement team, report to its lead and work in close collaboration with the Digital Finance team.

Your day moves between the FP&A teams, whose processes and knowledge you make machine-readable; the agents you design, build and improve; and the platforms they run on. What you learn flows back into our standards and into the enablement of the whole function.

How You’ll Make An Impact

- Design and build the FP&A context layer and its governance and operating model: extraction of knowledge from documents, processes and systems into structured, versioned context; the semantic layer with glossary, scopes and precedence rules; ownership, release and quality rules for every context asset
- Provide retrieval that every AI tool can consume – commodity solutions and self-built agents alike – with permissions intact and sources cited
- Make FP&A processes agentic: decompose redesigned processes into agent-executable steps with defined inputs, outputs, tools and boundaries, and build the agents that run them end to end
- Develop reusable agents, personas and templates for FP&A – in commodity solutions, with agent frameworks and as custom agents – and reference implementations the business teams can build on
- Advance harness engineering for our agents: tool and system integrations (finance data models, planning and reporting systems, Microsoft 365), execution environments, feedback loops and monitoring of behaviour and cost
- Deploy and operate your solutions along our governance framework for in-Finance-developed applications, in close collaboration with the Digital Finance team – with security, data privacy and Responsible AI by design
- Act as technical sparring partner for the FP&A community: answer questions on context, tools and agents, review what colleagues build and support them where they get stuck
- Scout and evaluate new tools, features and solutions, prototype quickly and recommend what FP&A should adopt
- Have fun building cool, innovative stuff with great people – and make sure the team around you has it too
