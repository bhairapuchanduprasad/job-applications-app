One of the companies we work with at Joppy is looking for a 
Forward Deployed Engineer to join the team.

Quality, speed and visible results matter from the first weeks.

• Evaluate the proposed solution and carry it through to delivery.

• Be the technical face of the project in front of the client, with constant, clear communication.

What we're looking for

• Real hands-on experience building with AI (LLMs, agents, automation) in production environments, not just courses or small personal projects.

• Intermediate level with at least one solid client engagement, up to senior or lead. This is not a position for junior profiles; there is real architecture involved.

• Someone who uses AI naturally in their day-to-day work (Claude Code, Codex or similar) and can defend the decisions they make with its help.

• Fluent English, to work comfortably and directly with a client team in the US.

• Autonomy, independent judgment and a genuine drive to own an entire project, not just execute tasks.

• Care for quality and attention to detail: work that is clean, well explained and delivered on time.

• Clear, proactive communication with both business stakeholders and engineers.

You don't need

A fixed tech stack. How you think and how you communicate matters far more to us than the exact list of technologies you know.
