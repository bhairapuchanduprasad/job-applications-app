About The Company

KI Group is a data-driven market intelligence and technology company serving customers across the plastics, chemicals, automotive, and pharmaceutical industries. Its roots date back to 1971, when it began as a specialist information service for the plastics industry. Today, KI Group combines sector expertise, journalism, software development, and data analysis to help businesses make informed decisions.

The company’s platforms provide market data, price indices, and analysis across areas such as commodity prices, supply chains, freight rates, energy, and raw materials. With pricenxt, KI Group has launched a new SaaS platform for pricing and market intelligence. This platform gives customers access to valuable proprietary data and creates a strong foundation for building practical, user-focused AI capabilities.

About The Role

As a (Senior) LLM Application Engineer, you will take ownership of KI Group’s LLM platform, developing AI capabilities from initial concept through production. You will begin by building features for pricenxt customers, who provide direct feedback as they use the product. Over time, your work will also support internal tools that help editorial and sales teams work more efficiently.

This is a hands-on engineering role for someone who has already delivered LLM features to real users. You will help shape technical direction, identify promising use cases, and ensure that AI products are useful, reliable, and grounded in the company’s market data. Your work will be visible in a newly launched product and will directly contribute to how customers access and act on complex industry information.

Qualifications

- Several years of professional software engineering experience, including at least one LLM-powered feature that you personally delivered to production and real users.
- Practical production experience with retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), including vector databases, retrieval strategies, chunking, evaluation, and grounding.
- Strong backend engineering skills in Python and/or TypeScript with Node.js, including service development, asynchronous workflows, API design, and data modelling.
- Hands-on experience with function calling, tool use, prompt engineering, and context engineering.
- The ability to work independently, make sound technical decisions, and explain those decisions clearly to non-technical colleagues.
- Very good German and English communication skills.
- Experience with foundation models such as Anthropic, OpenAI, or Mistral is valuable, as is familiarity with MCP, React, and frontend development.

Responsibilities

- Build and maintain the LLM platform layer, including retrieval services, embedding pipelines, and well-designed APIs.
- Develop and launch customer-facing AI capabilities, such as a conversational analyst for pricing data, semantic search, and intelligent alerts.
- Integrate foundation models and assess options based on response quality, cost, latency, and suitability for each use case.
- Design agent architectures and MCP interfaces that allow enterprise customers to access KI Group data from their own systems.
- Partner with colleagues to select use cases for production and measure their performance through evaluation, grounding, and monitoring.
- Apply strong engineering practices to create maintainable, secure, and dependable services that can evolve with customer needs.
- Use customer feedback and product outcomes to improve AI features and guide future development.
