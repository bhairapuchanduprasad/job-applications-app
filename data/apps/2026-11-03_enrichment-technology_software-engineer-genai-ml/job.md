About The Company

Enrichment Technology Company Limited (ETC) is an international high-tech company specializing in the development, manufacture, and installation of gas centrifuges for uranium enrichment. The company also supplies aluminium piping and delivers project management, plant design, and engineering services. As a highly specialized provider in the nuclear energy industry, ETC supports the generation of low-carbon energy around the world. Its work is guided by rigorous safety standards and a responsible approach to protecting sensitive technology.

Through its subsidiaries, ETC also serves other advanced technology markets. Pronexos provides specialized manufacturing products and services, particularly carbon-fibre products, while Stornetic develops flywheel systems for sustainable energy applications. ETC employs approximately 800 people across five locations in the United Kingdom, Germany, the Netherlands, France, and the United States. The company continues to grow in response to increasing global demand for low-carbon energy.

About The Role

Enrichment Technology Company Limited is seeking a Software Engineer – Generative AI & Machine Learning (m/f/d) to join its team in Jülich, Germany. In this role, you will design and build software applications and services that make artificial intelligence useful in specialist research, development, and manufacturing environments. Your work will span generative AI, AI agents, machine learning, and modern knowledge-management technologies.

You will collaborate with subject-matter experts, data scientists, IT teams, and information security colleagues to turn operational needs into practical, secure, and maintainable solutions. The role also contributes to the software operation of a modern AI high-performance computing centre and includes developing systems for local deployment and offline use.

Qualifications

- A completed degree, with a doctorate where applicable, in computer science, artificial intelligence, data science, engineering, mathematics, physics, or a comparable technical discipline; equivalent professional experience may also be considered.
- Two to five years of relevant professional experience, including hands-on development of software-based AI applications.
- Practical understanding of generative AI, machine learning, AI agents, and the integration of AI capabilities into software solutions.
- A structured, analytical, and independent approach to work, with the ability to investigate and resolve technical challenges.
- Strong communication and teamwork skills, particularly when working across disciplines and with stakeholders from different technical backgrounds.
- Good written and spoken English, along with an interest in continuously learning about emerging technologies.

Responsibilities

- Design, develop, and implement artificial intelligence applications and services, particularly in generative AI, AI agents, and machine learning.
- Build AI-supported workflows and incorporate modern knowledge-management technologies into suitable business processes.
- Develop APIs and microservices that integrate AI capabilities with existing applications and services.
- Implement access controls, permissions, logging, monitoring, and safeguards to support secure and reliable operation.
- Translate requirements from research, development, and manufacturing teams into appropriate AI-based solutions.
- Support the software operation of a modern AI high-performance computing centre.
- Maintain software quality through version control, systematic testing, clear documentation, and ongoing maintenance and improvement.
- Develop solutions that support offline operation and local deployment of AI systems.
- Follow company requirements for information security and data protection, working closely with relevant specialists.
