About The Role

We are looking for a Machine Learning Engineer to join the Estimations Department within our Data Science team, part of the Product and Technology group. Reporting to the Data Science Manager, you will collaborate with developers, engineers, analysts, and researchers to design, build, and productionize machine learning systems.

You will contribute to solutions for complex logistics challenges, helping balance supply and demand and improve the efficiency, reliability, and profitability of our delivery service. Your work may support applications such as estimated delivery times, logistics optimization, and pricing. You will take ideas from experimentation through implementation and deployment, ensuring that models and data science applications perform effectively in real-world product environments. This is a full-time hybrid role based in Berlin, with three office days and two home-working days each week.

Qualifications

You bring strong machine learning experience, including developing solutions for forecasting, logistics, or related products. You are proficient in Python and have solid software engineering skills, with the ability to work independently and solve complex technical problems. You have experience productizing and deploying machine learning applications using cloud services such as Amazon Web Services or Google Cloud Platform.

You are familiar with microservices, event-driven architectures, serverless computing, and cloud architecture patterns. Experience deploying workloads to Kubernetes is expected. Knowledge of tools such as Airflow, MLflow, Docker, or Flink is an advantage. Experience operating real-time production systems at scale, including services handling millions of requests or transactions per day, is desirable. You work effectively with multidisciplinary teams and are committed to sharing knowledge and promoting sound engineering practices.

Responsibilities

Research, implement, and deploy machine learning techniques that address logistics and estimation challenges. Develop and scale solutions that improve decision-making across relevant product use cases, and translate promising approaches into robust production systems. Build and maintain data science applications that integrate with customer-facing and operational products.

Create and improve machine learning pipelines, data tools, and evaluation frameworks that support experimentation, iteration, and reliable launches. Monitor model and application performance, identify opportunities to improve efficiency and quality, and work with partner teams to deliver solutions that meet product and operational needs. Contribute to technical discussions, share expertise, and help establish practices that support maintainable, effective machine learning systems.

Benefits

Join an international technology organization where you can work on complex, high-impact problems alongside experienced colleagues. The role offers a hybrid working arrangement, opportunities to collaborate across functions, and an environment that encourages learning, knowledge sharing, and professional growth. You will contribute to products used by customers and partners across a global platform, while working in a supportive, fast-paced team that recognizes shared progress and success.
