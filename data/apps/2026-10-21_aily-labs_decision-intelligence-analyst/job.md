# Decision Intelligence Analyst
Aily Labs

## Mission
As a Decision Intelligence Analyst, you will be at the heart of how Aily's platform comes to life for our clients. You will own the day-to-day execution of client integrations: preparing and managing data, running and optimizing AI models, and ensuring that every tenant delivers the highest possible quality and business impact.

## Your profile
Hands-on, execution-focused role for someone analytical, detail-oriented and excited about making AI work in the real world. Works closely with an AI Delivery Lead and across client-facing and technical teams to turn business needs into working, high-impact AI solutions.

## What you'll do
- Prepare, structure, and upload client data into Aily's proprietary data models, ensuring accuracy, quality, and completeness
- Adapt and configure data models to fit specific client contexts, business processes, and decision workflows
- Run and operate AI models end-to-end, taking ownership of model performance and output quality across client tenants
- Execute model fine-tuning and optimization cycles to continuously improve decision outcomes and business relevance
- Monitor and validate AI outputs on an ongoing basis, identifying inconsistencies and proactively resolving quality issues
- Develop a deep understanding of each client's business routines, user workflows, and decision-making processes
- Track and report on tenant health metrics, surfacing insights and recommendations to maximize client impact
- Collaborate with the AI Delivery Lead, Product, and Engineering teams to flag issues, share learnings, and contribute to platform improvements

## What you bring
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Data Science, Mathematics, Statistics, or related
- Proficiency in Python and SQL; solid understanding of data structures and modeling concepts
- Strong attention to detail, structured problem-solving, drive to get things done with high quality
- Comfortable taking responsibility for the quality and performance of the solutions you manage
- Eager to deepen expertise in AI decision systems, model fine-tuning, and agentic AI architectures
- Clear communicator across technical and non-technical stakeholders
- Able to context-switch across industries, business functions, and client environments
- Genuine interest in how end users interact with AI in their daily work

Bonus: scikit-learn, PyTorch or similar; AWS, GCP or Azure; internship/work experience in data, analytics or AI; exposure to enterprise software implementations or client-facing technical roles.

## Who we are
Aily Labs builds the AI operating system for business decisions, working with leading enterprises across industries. Competitive salary and equity; flexible, remote-first culture with offices in Lisbon and Munich; ownership, curiosity, direct feedback.
