About GlassFlow
GlassFlow builds data infrastructure for AI agents in production, featuring two core products: GlassFlow Tares, which feeds agents correlated data across systems, and GlassFlow Rius, which traces and debugs agent behavior. Operating as a Berlin startup backed by Upfront Ventures, the CEO of GitHub, and the ex-CTO of Aiven, the team comprises serial entrepreneurs with over 10 years of experience.

What you will do
As a Founding Engineer, you will shape the platform's technical foundation in a hybrid role based in Berlin. You will collaborate directly with the founders to build and scale the infrastructure supporting AI agents.

- 
Product Integration for Client Use Cases
- 
Go-to Person for technical questions for the internal sales and marketing team
- 
Building and developing the connector and ingestion layer
- 
Part of the core development team
- 
Creation of product tutorials and guides
