Job Purpose/role

Allianz Investment Management (AIM) is the internal investment manager of the Allianz Group. We develop and implement the investment strategy for Allianz insurance entities worldwide and manage the risk-return profile of the Group’s investment portfolio in a sustainable and responsible way.

Within AIM, the Digital Solutions for Investment (DSI) team develops and delivers digital, data and AI solutions supporting teams across the global investment value chain. In your role, you will work closely with internal and external stakeholders to translate business needs into practical technical solutions. You will combine hands-on development with project coordination and stakeholder collaboration, contributing to the development, implementation and adoption of AI, GenAI and analytics solutions across AIM.

Key Responsibilities

-  Work with stakeholders across AIM, external vendors and other Allianz entities to identify use cases and translate business requirements into technical solutions
-  Contribute to AI, GenAI, analytics and data projects from concept to implementation, take responsibility for defined workstreams and present results and recommendations to AIM management
-  Develop and enhance applications as part of the engineering team, turning ideas and prototypes into practical solutions
-  Support GenAI adoption across AIM by developing training and enablement formats such as workshops and town halls
-  Contribute to AIM’s AI and Analytics governance in line with regulatory and Allianz Group requirements and evaluate emerging technologies and use cases

Key Requirements/skills/ Experience

-  Up to 5 years of professional experience in a technical role, ideally at the intersection of business and technology
-  Master’s degree in computer science, data science, (financial) mathematics, business informatics or a related field
-  Strong Python skills and hands-on experience with GenAI applications, such as LLMs, RAG or agent frameworks, as well as data engineering and analytics
-  Experience with Azure or AWS and containerized applications such as Docker or Kubernetes; Infrastructure as Code, e.g. Terraform, is a plus
-  Strong analytical and problem-solving skills combined with effective communication and stakeholder management
-  Knowledge of investment or risk management and AI governance or regulation, e.g. the EU AI Act, is a plus
-  Fluent English; German is a plus

What We Offer

- Our buddy program, a welcome event and a structured onboarding to help you get started with AIM.
- A broad range of learning opportunities will help you staying relevant in your current role and growing into future workplace demands
- An open and international working environment with a diverse and inclusive culture will inspire your everyday work. A working model which balances remote and office based work will give you the flexibility to organize the way you work.
- A company pension scheme, well-being initiatives, sports offers and other local benefits will allow you achieving a positive work life balance
