Product Engineer (Client Platform)

(€70K – €95K)

We're looking for a Product Engineer to develop our Client Platform: A system to handle and allow our Clients to operate all of their work easily with us.

The goal is to build a minimal, usable platform that lets clients do every important action themselves. It automates what can be automated and makes the rest easy to see and act on.

Our Client Platform sits at the core of our business, so what you build will directly shape how our clients work with us every day.
