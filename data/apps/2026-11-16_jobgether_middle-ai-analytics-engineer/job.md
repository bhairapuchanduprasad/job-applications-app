This position is listed on behalf of a partner company, which manages all applications and next steps. Our partner is looking for a Middle AI Analytics Engineer based in Germany.

We are looking for a skilled and innovative Middle AI Analytics Engineer to strengthen a growing Data & Analytics team within an international technology environment. In this fully remote role, you will help transform the way analytical teams access, process, and leverage data through AI-powered solutions. You will focus on improving self-service analytics, automating repetitive analytical workflows, and developing robust frameworks to evaluate AI performance and reliability. Working with technologies such as Python, SQL, Claude, RAG, and BI platforms, you will build practical solutions that enhance productivity and decision-making. You will also contribute to advancing AI-assisted analytics through context engineering, intelligent automation, and continuous quality monitoring. This is an excellent opportunity to work on ambitious technical projects, influence AI adoption, and grow within a collaborative, innovation-driven organization.

Accountabilities

- Self-Service Analytics Enhancement: Improve the quality and accessibility of self-service analytics through Metabase by developing and integrating AI skills, contextual information, and intelligent workflows that help users retrieve accurate and relevant insights.
- Analytics Workflow Automation: Identify and automate repetitive tasks within the Data & Analytics team, developing AI-powered tools such as automated code reviewers, reusable AI skills, and other productivity-enhancing solutions.
- AI Quality Evaluation: Design and implement a comprehensive framework for evaluating the quality, accuracy, and reliability of AI-driven solutions used by the analytics team.
- Claude and Context Engineering: Design and optimize Claude skills, including their purpose, instructions, examples, and contextual information, to improve response quality and minimize hallucinations.
- RAG Development and Optimization: Design, implement, and improve Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems, including source indexing, document chunking, information retrieval, and reranking strategies.
- AI Performance Monitoring: Develop evaluation methodologies using golden answers, automated evaluations (evals), LLM-as-a-judge techniques, regression testing, and performance monitoring to identify quality issues and detect model drift.
- Root Cause Analysis: Investigate AI-related performance issues, identify underlying causes, and implement improvements to ensure reliable and consistent analytical outputs.
- Cross-Functional Collaboration: Work closely with analytics and technical teams to identify opportunities for AI adoption, improve existing processes, and deliver scalable solutions that support business objectives.
- Continuous Innovation: Explore new AI capabilities, tools, and development approaches to improve analytical efficiency and encourage the adoption of intelligent automation across the organization.

Requirements

- Practical experience in AI engineering, analytics engineering, data engineering, or a related technical role.
- Strong SQL skills, with the ability to query, manipulate, and analyze structured data effectively.
- Proficiency in Python for developing automation solutions, analytical tools, and AI-powered applications.
- Hands-on experience with Claude, Claude Code, and Model Context Protocol (MCP).
- Strong understanding of Claude skills and context engineering, including designing reusable skills, defining clear instructions, providing relevant examples, and optimizing prompt context to reduce hallucinations.
- Experience designing and improving Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems, including document indexing, chunking strategies, retrieval optimization, and reranking.
- Knowledge of AI evaluation methodologies, including golden datasets or answers, evaluation frameworks, LLM-as-a-judge, regression testing, model drift monitoring, and root cause analysis.
- Ability to assess the quality, reliability, and effectiveness of AI-generated outputs and continuously improve AI-powered solutions.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving abilities, with a structured approach to investigating technical challenges.
- Good communication and collaboration skills, with the ability to exchange ideas, share feedback, and work effectively within a multidisciplinary team.
- A proactive and adaptable mindset, with a willingness to experiment, take ownership, and continuously develop technical expertise.
- Nice to have: Experience developing AI agents through LLM APIs or SDKs, including tool integration, context management, and cost optimization.
- Familiarity with Business Intelligence platforms and their role as data access layers for AI systems, including data models, metrics, and query structures. Experience with Metabase or Tableau is an advantage.
- Previous experience working with dbt for data transformation and analytics engineering.
