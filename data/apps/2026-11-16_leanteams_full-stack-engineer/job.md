Fullstack Software Engineer (Backend-Focused)
We are hiring for an ambitious AI startup entering an exciting growth phase.

About the opportunity
This is an opportunity to join a fast-growing startup building the next generation of AI-powered research and knowledge tools.
The product combines Agentic AI, Machine Learning, and collaborative research, allowing users to discover information, analyze complex sources, organize knowledge, and work alongside intelligent AI agents in a single platform.
With international ambitions, an evolving product, and an upcoming fundraising round, the startup is entering an exciting stage of development.
They are looking for an experienced Fullstack Software Engineer with strong backend expertise who enjoys solving complex technical challenges, building scalable systems, and taking real ownership of their work.
The role offers the opportunity to work closely with the founders and a small engineering team, contribute to major technical and product decisions, and have a direct impact on the product's evolution.

What you'll work on
•⁠ ⁠Build across the stack: Design and implement robust backend systems, APIs, data models, and frontend features.
•⁠ ⁠Develop complex product features: Build research libraries, collaborative workspaces, social functionalities, and workflows connecting users with intelligent AI agents.
•⁠ ⁠Design for reliability and scalability: Improve system performance, optimize databases, investigate failures, and make thoughtful architectural decisions.
•⁠ ⁠Integrate Agentic AI and Machine Learning: Work with Python-based ML components, AI services, and agent-driven workflows to develop intelligent product experiences.
•⁠ ⁠Own features end to end: From understanding the problem and designing the architecture to implementation, testing, and deployment.
•⁠ ⁠Contribute to product decisions: Collaborate with the founders and engineering team to shape the product's technical direction.

The tech stack
The platform is primarily built with TypeScript, using:
•⁠ ⁠Backend: Node.js, Fastify, PostgreSQL, queues
•⁠ ⁠Frontend: SvelteKit, Tailwind CSS, DaisyUI
•⁠ ⁠Testing & Desktop: Playwright, Electron
•⁠ ⁠Architecture: Domain-Driven Design (DDD), CQRS
•⁠ ⁠AI & Machine Learning: Python-based ML components, AI services, and integrations with intelligent agents

The technology stack continues to evolve. Strong engineering fundamentals, adaptability, and technical judgment matter more than experience with every individual tool.
