What You'll Build

You will help build the foundation that turns manual engineering work into smart, automated AI agents/ workflows. Your work removes friction for other engineers by making common capabilities reusable instead of reimplemented over and over again.

You’ll contribute to a shared platform that enables teams to create and extend AI‑powered agents with much less effort. This helps engineers spend more time solving real problems and less time dealing with plumbing and setup.

The impact is immediate and visible. Better automation means faster investigations, more reliable systems, and fewer interruptions in daily engineering work. You help teams trust their tools, scale their solutions, and work more efficiently every day.

Where you belong
