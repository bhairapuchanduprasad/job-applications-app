Intro

carbmee works with the largest companies in automotive, defense and industrials on some of their hardest data problems. Their transactional data, bills of materials and unstructured data sit in different systems and rarely talk to each other.

The EIS, the Environmental Intelligence System, connects them at item level and runs AI on top: matching items to emission factors, checking data quality and recommending reductions. Where a standard setup isn’t enough, we build custom solutions with the customer. ZF, ZEISS and Maersk use carbmee to make sourcing, product and compliance decisions with numbers they can defend.

The role

Our customers don’t buy software and figure it out alone. You go in with it.

As a Forward Deployed Engineer for North America, you own the technical side of our accounts with the largest US and Canadian companies in automotive, defense and industrials. You’ll be among the first on the ground for carbmee in the region, so there’s no ready-made playbook. You’ll help write it. You support the deal, lead the implementation and configure the solution to the customer’s specific needs.

Expect complex ERP landscapes, global supply chains with tens of thousands of suppliers, and executives who want answers, not dashboards. What you learn there goes straight back into our product.
