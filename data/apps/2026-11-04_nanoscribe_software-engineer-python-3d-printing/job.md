Job Description

The software engineering team at Nanoscribe develops software for controlling high-precision 3D laser printers. The software is a stack of modules that covers everything from a touch-based user interface to numerical algorithms and 3D geometry processing to the control of different hardware components such as sensors, cameras, etc. The software stack follows a layered client/server architecture, with different communication protocols connecting the layers.

As part of our software engineering team, you will work closely with engineers and domain experts on various R&D projects. You will have the chance to bring in your own ideas and contribute to product definitions.

Your Day-to-day Tasks Will Include

- Discussing and clarifying requirements with our internal domain experts
- Breaking requirements down into manageable work packages, estimating effort, and identifying technical dependencies
- Designing and developing algorithms that span and are optimized across various layers of our software stack
- Designing and developmenting software that interacts with specific hardware components
- Continuously optimizing the functionality and quality of the software for better scalability, maintainability and performance

Your profile

- You have successfully completed your studies in computer science, mathematics, physics, or engineering

- You are fluent in Python and can find your way in a large codebase
- You are familiar with AI-assisted development workflows and best practices for productive, responsible use of AI coding tools
- Experience with numerical algorithms, GPU graphics, and computational geometry on 3D meshes is a plus
- Experience with near-hardware programming, such as interfacing to cameras and sensors, or FPGA development is a plus
- Knowledge of software security principles and secure coding practices is a plus
- You are motivated, enjoy working in a team, and appreciate clean code
