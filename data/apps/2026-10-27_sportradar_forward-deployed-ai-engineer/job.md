Job Description

ABOUT US: 

Sportradar is a sports data and technology company. As Forward Deployed AI Engineer, you work like an internal consultant: you are placed in a department or business unit, talk with the people there, find the real problems, cut through complexity, simplify how work is done, and then build and ship LLM and agent-based solutions. You work largely independently with little day-to-day supervision, find your way through organisational complexity and competing priorities to get things done, and learn fast. Senior engineers are available for guidance.

THE CHALLENGE:

- Talk with colleagues in your department to understand workflows, pain points and data.
- Identify problems worth solving and break unclear or cluttered situations into clear, manageable steps.
- Streamline and simplify processes, and propose where an AI solution makes sense and where it does not.
- Work with several stakeholders and competing priorities, and keep your work moving pragmatically.
- Build, test and deploy LLM-powered applications and agents, from prototype to production.
- Implement retrieval (RAG), tool integrations, prompts and context handling.
- Build and run evaluations (test sets, automated checks, quality dashboards) and use the results to improve solutions.
- Build and maintain data pipelines that feed these systems.
- Plan your own work, give regular updates and document solutions.

ABOUT YOU: 

- Around 2+ years of software engineering or applied ML experience, or equivalent project experience.
- Clear communication with non-technical colleagues; able to explain technical topics simply.
- Self-motivation and the ability to work independently with little supervision.
- Pragmatic approach to organisational complexity and competing priorities.
- Structured approach to finding problems and simplifying complexity.
- Solid Python and software engineering fundamentals (testing, version control, APIs).
- Hands-on experience with LLM APIs and a project using RAG or agents, including some evaluation.
- Familiarity with SQL or data pipelines and with a cloud platform (AWS, Azure or GCP).

Nice to have

- Consulting, internship or stakeholder-facing project experience.

Working model

Based in Leipzig. Some travel between Sportradar offices may be required.
