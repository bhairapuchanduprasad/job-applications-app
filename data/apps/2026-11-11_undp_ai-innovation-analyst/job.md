Job Description

Background

The United Nations Development Programme (UNDP) Unit of Information and Technology Management (ITM) in New York are the enablers supporting UNDP Country Offices around the world with technological solutions that empower the projects we deliver. Our Unit offers a stimulating and versatile internship in an international environment with contact to various partners from some 166 countries with UNDP presence. We are a team who work in a dynamic and informal atmosphere, and we expect our interns to become vital members of the team.

UNDP is committed to leveraging artificial intelligence (AI) as a transformative tool to advance sustainable development objectives. The AI, Data and Engineering Team works across divisions to design and implement cutting-edge AI solutions tailored to the unique challenges faced by global communities. This dynamic role facilitates innovation and AI-based insights that help achieve the organization’s strategic goals.

The AI Innovation Analyst is responsible for contributing to the development and implementation of AI-based solutions, fostering a culture of technological innovation, and ensuring adherence to ethical AI principles within UNDP’s global operations.

Duties And Responsibilities

1- Contribute to the AI Solutions Development:

- Support in designing AI tools and platforms that address corporate operation challenges.
- Contribute to the deployment of AI systems to optimize operational efficiency and business processes.

2- Contribute To The Strategic Innovation

- Collaborate with business counterparts to identify new AI opportunities.
- Contribute to building frameworks for AI-driven decision-making processes.

3- Ensure Ethical Compliance

- Ensure all AI initiatives align with international ethical standards and UNDP policies.
- Monitor AI implementations for potential risks and mitigate concerns proactively.

4- Facilitate Capacity Building and knowledge sharing

- Facilitate knowledge-sharing activities and conduct training workshops for stakeholders to understand AI applications.
- Work with partners to scale emerging AI innovations and foster collaboration and support capacity building 

The incumbent performs other duties within their functional profile as deemed necessary for the efficient functioning of the Office and the Organization.

Institutional Arrangement

On a day-to-day basis, the AI Innovation Specialist will be working with and directly reporting to the Chief of AI, Data and Engineering.

Education

Required Skills and Experience

- Advanced university degree (master’s degree or equivalent) in Artificial Intelligence, Computer Science, Data Science, or related field is required. Or

Experience

- Minimum of 2 years (with master’s degree) or 4 years (with bachelor’s degree) of relevant work experience, preferably in the area of in Artificial Intelligence, Computer Science, Data Science, or related fields, ideally within an international or UN environment is required.
