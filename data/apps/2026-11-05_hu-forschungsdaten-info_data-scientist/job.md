# Data Scientist (m/f/d, E13 TV-L, 100%) — Cluster of Excellence HUMAN ORIGINS (forschungsdaten.info), Tübingen
Deadline: 15.11.2026. Start 01.01.2027, fixed-term until 31.12.2032.

The Cluster's Research Data Management Unit (jointly led by QBiC and the Digital Humanities Center) seeks a Data Scientist to complement the unit's data stewards.

Duties:
* Building, testing, and deploying custom research software, web applications, and interactive dashboards/portals to facilitate cluster-wide data exploration, integration, and visualization.
* Developing and applying state-of-the-art artificial intelligence, machine learning, or NLP solutions to extract patterns, classify artifacts/data, and support hypotheses in and across human origins research projects.
* Designing, implementing, and maintaining scalable analytical pipelines and workflows for heterogeneous, multimodal datasets (e.g., 3D scans, ancient genomics, archaeological spatial data, and text corpora).
* Developing interfaces between the research infrastructure of QBiC and DHC to ensure interoperability of computational environments and tools.
* Partnering closely with the Cluster's PIs and research teams to translate complex research questions into robust computational methods and contribute to scientific publications.
* Establishing and applying best practices for Research Software Engineering, including version control, containerization, automated testing, and open-science compliance.

Profile:
* Master's or equivalent, preferably doctorate, in computer science, data science or related field. Humanities background a plus.
* Experience applying data science methods and tools in research projects.
* Strong software development and web engineering skills.
* Experience developing and applying AI solutions in data science and software engineering.
* Openness to interdisciplinary data science; strong organizational and communication skills; autonomous work in an international environment.
* Very good English, written and spoken. (German not mentioned.)

Application: cover letter, CV, certificates as a single PDF (max 10 MB) by email. No named contact person.
