About SoccerSolver
SoccerSolver is an AI-powered player valuation and transfer market intelligence platform for professional football clubs. We were finalists at the MIT Sloan Sports Analytics Conference 2026 and are building a family of verticals: AgentSolver, MediaSolver, BrandSolver, ValueSolver and xV.

At SoccerSolver every team member is a partner in the company: you share in its success and you are part of its growth. It is hard to get in and hard to leave.

The role
We are looking for an Agentic AI Engineer to design, build and ship AI agents that run real parts of our business: executive and sales copilots, social media agents focused on lead generation, outbound agents that open new markets, and decision agents that turn football questions into real optimization problems.

What you will do
• Build agents end to end: orchestrator, tools and MCP, persistent memory and state.
• Make them proactive: triggered by schedules and events (a match, a transfer, an unanswered email), not only by prompts.
• Keep humans in the loop: approval queues and guardrails so irreversible actions never run unchecked.
• Instrument everything: decision logs, tool calls and cost per run.
• Prove they work: evaluation sets and backtests on past seasons.
• Work with real public football data and cite every source.

What we look for
• Strong Python and hands-on experience shipping LLM agents to production.
• Experience with agent frameworks, tool calling and MCP, retrieval and memory.
• Solid data engineering: messy sources, entity resolution across providers, APIs.
• Background in optimization, statistics or ML is a strong plus.
• Football knowledge and judgment about what matters for a football business.
• Ownership: you would rather do it well than fast, and you are still fast.

How to apply
Apply here. Shortlisted candidates will receive our technical challenge: build one working agent from a set of ideas and walk us through it in a short video. We value real working software over polished slides.
