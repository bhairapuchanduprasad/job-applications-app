Join us in making a difference

As a Software Engineer in our AI & Productivity Solutions team, you'll turn the everyday needs of colleagues across trivago (we call them talents) into shipped software. We built trivago Copilot, the Help Center smart search and the Business Studio chatbot, and we're now working on Claude automation, the "trivago brain" (our memory layer for AI) and a web app for our MCP and Skills Library. Our team of developers, QA, Product Owners, Project Managers and AI Strategy Managers delivers most of trivago's internal AI productivity tools, in a field that changes weekly. You'll own initiatives end to end, shape the technical approach, and mentor others as we grow. We move fast and hold high standards, and we invest just as much in each other's growth, so no one figures it out alone. If that pace energizes you, apply and help shape how trivago works with AI.

How you’ll make an impact:

- Independently drive initiatives from prototype to production, building apps, integrations, copilots and chatbots for internal AI and productivity needs. 
- Write clean, tested and maintainable code, and champion best practices and secure development principles across our connectors to SaaS and custom tools. 
- Understand the business value behind every request: talk to talents about their workflows, advise them on technical opportunities, and turn their needs into solutions that matter. 
- Contribute to architecture and technology discussions, evaluating alternative approaches and choosing the best fit for a fast-evolving AI stack together with our Product Owners. 
- Share your work and learnings through Show & Tells, technical documentation and post-mortems, and guide less experienced engineers along the way. 
- Stay close to the AI frontier by keeping up with new models, frameworks and best practices, and bring fresh approaches into the team's work. 

What you'll need to thrive:

- Strong full-stack engineering experience: you build APIs, integrations and data-backed features and ship maintainable software end to end, ideally with C#/.NET, React/TypeScript and Azure or similar cloud services (strong fundamentals in any modern stack carry over). 
- A pragmatic, product-minded approach: you start from the problem, ship the smallest valuable version fast, iterate, and care whether what you built actually made someone's day easier. 
- A collaborative, consultative style: you enjoy working with people across the company to figure out what's worth building, and you stay productive when priorities shift and there's no fixed six-month roadmap. 
- A genuine learning mindset: you approach challenges with curiosity, seek out feedback, and pick up new tools and frameworks quickly. 
- A performance mindset: you set ambitious goals for yourself and have the determination and resilience to follow through, even when the ground moves under you. 
- An open and curious mindset toward AI: you experiment constantly, use AI to deliver real outcomes rather than demos, and apply the right guardrails for data access and privacy. 

Not ticking every box? We still want to hear from you. Apply, tell us what motivates you, and you could be a great match—now or in the future. Your application does not need to include a photo.
