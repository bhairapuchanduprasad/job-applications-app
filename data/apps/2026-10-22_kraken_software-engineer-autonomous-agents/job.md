# Software Engineer (Autonomous Agents)
Kraken (Kraken Technologies) · Berlin

AI is a key investment area for Kraken Technologies. You'll join Autonomous Agents, the team building AI-powered customer service for the energy industry. We connect conversational AI to Kraken's customer platform so agents can use account information and approved tools to resolve real customer needs, like understanding a bill or submitting a meter reading, and hand over to a person, with full context, when it matters. The team is moving from initial trials to long-term production use, and scaling from client-specific builds to a reusable product that can serve many clients.

## Where you'll fit in
Cross-functional team of ~10 engineers plus product and ML specialists, working with Kraken platform teams.
- **Build:** reusable core: client-agnostic agent journeys and tooling, MCP interfaces into Kraken, email/messaging/voice, observability, feature toggles and guardrails.
- **Implementation:** taking the core to individual clients: understand their customer journeys, configure and extend, feed learnings back.

## What you'll own
- Investigate customer support queries, regulatory and business processes; turn them into technical plans.
- Build integrations between Kraken, Model Context Protocol (MCP) interfaces and the AI agent setup: Python on the Kraken side, TypeScript on the agent side.
- Turn patterns into reusable, client-agnostic components.
- Authentication, permissions, validation and human handoff in the design; test and monitor non-deterministic behaviour.
- Ship features behind toggles, tested and documented.
- Client conversations, clear documentation, follow-through.
- Dashboards, monitoring and runbooks.

## What you bring
- Shipped and supported production software; testing, code review, team codebase.
- Python and TypeScript.
- Problem-first thinking.
- Curiosity about LLMs, autonomous agents and MCP; hands-on experience is great.
- Comfortable talking to non-engineers; translating messy real-world processes into software.
- Organised, clear documentation, follow-through.
- Learning agility.

## Bonus
AI engineering (LLMs, agent frameworks, MCP servers); conversational AI (customer service automation, voice, messaging); evals and monitoring of non-deterministic systems; Django; Datadog, Sentry; client-facing technical delivery.
